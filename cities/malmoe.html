Malmö

Malmö is the capital and largest city of the Swedish county of Skåne. It is known for its vibrant cultural scene, beautiful archipelago, and historic architecture.

About Malmö

Malmö is a dynamic city that seamlessly blends its rich history with a modern, vibrant atmosphere. Known for its stunning archipelago, world-class museums, and thriving arts scene, Malmö offers visitors a unique glimpse into Swedish culture and innovation.
